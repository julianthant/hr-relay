# UCPath store

Tasks use `driver-declaration.ts`; raw browser operations stay under `driver/`. Locator recipes
live in `driver/ui-registry.ts` and need fixture plus live-read-only evidence. Person Org and Smart
HR share a system and declaration, not routes, frames, or anchors; scope each guard to its surface.

## Termination and sweep invariants

- Prepare stages the whole Smart HR wizard; commit performs the named irreversible action. Smart HR
  uses page scope, not the legacy iframe funnel. A pre-save `NEW` ID is not a transaction receipt.
- Prove the subject through UCPath's resolved displayed name; reading the typed EID back only echoes
  input. Missing person resolution refuses before a write. Keep identity checks bound to the run.
- Recognize both verified termination labels (`TER` and `Terminatn`); blank matches neither.
- Sweep only Pending/Pushed Back rows. Bind the complete eligible/protected set to stable T IDs,
  re-read before staging, and uniquely join the other grid by its verified identity tuple.
  Positional DOM suffixes are never identity. Changed, missing, extra, or ambiguous rows refuse
  before fencing/clicking. Verify selected rows absent and protected rows preserved.
- Keep the exact-set digest and v2 proof; historical observation-only receipts remain immutable.
  A transaction number alone is not a receipt: verify its status and exact subject/date/action.
- Before Kuali finalization, always re-run the fresh exact-T-ID filing-presence read. It grants
  current-presence authority only; an asserted receipt never becomes controlled-filing evidence.
- Post-fence absence is operator-only unless the owning contract has accepted a measured replacement.
  Never guess a propagation window or retry an UNKNOWN commit.
